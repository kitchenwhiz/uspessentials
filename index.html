    Shipping

    Prices cover material and standard packaging. You choose the carrier and pay freight; we load at our Bommasandra facility.

    Unloading and placement at your site are your responsibility. Check goods on delivery and note any damage on the carrier's receipt before signing.

    Warranty and returns

    12 months against manufacturing defects from the invoice date. Defects found in the first 15 days get a free replacement; after that, free repair.

    Valid returns (defects or wrong supply) raised within 7 days get a credit note usable for 6 months. No cash refunds.
